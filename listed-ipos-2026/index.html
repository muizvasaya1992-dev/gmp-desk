Common questions

What does listing-day gain mean?

It compares the share's close on the first trading day with the IPO issue price. A positive figure means it closed above the issue price.

Does the listing-day result show how the share did later?

No. It covers the first day only. Prices keep moving after listing.

Where does this data come from?

Listing details come from NSE and BSE. Some IPOs have no listing-day figure yet.
