Common questions

What is a Mainboard IPO?

A Mainboard IPO is a public issue by a larger company that lists on the main boards of NSE and BSE. Retail investors can bid for up to ₹2 lakh in one application.

How do I apply?

Open the IPO section of your broker or bank app, pick the IPO, choose the number of lots and enter your UPI ID. Approve the payment request in your UPI app before the cut-off on the last day. The money stays blocked in your account until allotment. IPO Shop does not take applications.

What does subscription mean?

Subscription shows how many times the shares on offer have been bid for. 5x means investors asked for five times the shares available. It measures demand and does not predict the listing price.
