Common questions

How are the price band and lot size decided?

The company announces the price band a few days before the IPO opens. The lot size is the minimum number of shares you must bid for, so the minimum investment is the lot size times the price.

How do I apply when an IPO opens?

Open the IPO section of your broker or bank app, pick the IPO, choose the number of lots and enter your UPI ID. Approve the payment request in your UPI app before the cut-off on the last day. The money stays blocked in your account until allotment. IPO Shop does not take applications.

Where do the dates come from?

Dates, price band and lot size come from NSE and BSE and are refreshed automatically. Always confirm in the offer document (RHP).
