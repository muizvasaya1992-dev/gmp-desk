Good to know

GMP (grey market premium) is unofficial, comes from informal markets and is not regulated by SEBI or the exchanges. It changes quickly and often differs from the actual listing price.

IPO Shop is an information website. It does not sell, apply for or recommend IPOs, and nothing here is investment advice. Read the offer document (RHP) and speak to a SEBI-registered advisor before investing.
