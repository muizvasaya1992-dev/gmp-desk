Good to know

GMP (grey market premium) is unofficial, comes from informal markets and is not regulated by SEBI or the exchanges. It changes quickly and often differs from the actual listing price. This site shows data for information only and is not investment advice. Read the offer document (RHP) and speak to a SEBI-registered advisor before investing.
