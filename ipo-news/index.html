Common questions

Where do these updates come from?

Each line is written from figures this site records: dates and subscription from NSE and BSE, GMP from public trackers, and listing-day prices from the exchanges. Nothing here is opinion or a tip.

How often is this page updated?

The data behind it refreshes about every 20 minutes on weekdays, and the page is rebuilt whenever something changes.

Do you publish IPO news stories?

No. We publish what the numbers did, with the date. For company news and analysis, read the offer document and the business press.
