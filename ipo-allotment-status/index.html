Common questions

How do I check my IPO allotment status?

Open the registrar's IPO allotment page named for your IPO, choose the IPO, and enter your PAN, application number or demat (DP) ID. Your broker app may also show the result.

When will I get the shares or the refund?

Allotment is usually finalised a day after the IPO closes. Shares are credited to your demat account and unused money is released, usually about a day before listing.

What if I did not get any shares?

Many IPOs are oversubscribed, so not every applicant is allotted shares. The money blocked through UPI is released after allotment.
