Common questions

What is GMP in an IPO?

GMP, the grey market premium, is an unofficial price in the grey market. The exchanges do not publish it, it can change quickly, and it often differs from the real listing price.

Does a high GMP guarantee a listing gain?

No. The GMP can fall or rise before listing, and the final price depends on how the shares trade on the first day.

Where does the GMP on IPO Shop come from?

GMP figures come from public trackers and our own checks. They are unofficial and shown for information only.
